Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

Verbo works without an account. There are no ads, analytics or tracking. Some features connect directly to Apple, Wikimedia and GitHub; those services process the information needed to provide the feature, as described below.

What we collect

Verbo has no developer-run servers and we don’t collect, store or sell your saved words, practice history or language choices. A word request is sent to the public dictionary services below, and Apple processes iCloud sync, speech recognition and purchases. Those services handle information under their own privacy policies.

What stays on your iPhone

Your settings, Logbook, practice progress and streak are stored on your iPhone. Verbo’s widgets read the shared app data needed to show your words and progress. Removing the app removes local data; iCloud data is managed separately by Apple.

iCloud Sync

iCloud Sync is on by default. Your saved words, review progress and learning statistics are stored in your private iCloud database and can sync to your other Apple devices. The developer cannot access this private database. Sync requires an iCloud account; you can turn it off in Settings. Apple’s privacy policy applies.

Services Verbo connects to

To find new words, meanings and pronunciations, Verbo connects directly to free, public services:

  • Wiktionary and Wikimedia Commons, run by the Wikimedia Foundation, for meanings, examples and native-speaker recordings. The word being loaded or looked up is sent to them.
  • GitHub, for public lists of common words in each language.

Like any website, they can see your device’s IP address when Verbo connects, but they receive nothing that identifies you personally. Their own policies apply: Wikimedia and GitHub.

Microphone and speech

Verbo only uses the microphone during Speak practice, after you tap the microphone button. Apple’s speech recognition turns your voice into text to check your pronunciation, and Apple may process it under its privacy policy. Verbo never records, stores or sends your voice anywhere else.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. No notification data leaves your device.

Purchases

Verbo Premium is sold by Apple. Apple handles payment, and we never see your name, card or Apple Account. The app only learns whether a purchase is active.

Children

Verbo does not ask for a name, email address or account and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Dictionary entries are user-contributed and may vary in subject matter.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here and in the app, with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: verboapp.team@outlook.com. If you choose to email us, we receive your address and message only if you send it. Verbo adds its app version and your iOS version to the draft; word reports may also include the word, language and meaning shown so we can investigate.

Help

Restoring Premium

Bought Premium on another device, or reinstalled Verbo? Open Settings in Verbo and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Cancel at least a day before renewal and Premium stays on until the end of the period you’ve paid for. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds and usually replies within 48 hours. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and choose Verbo.

Adding a widget

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Verbo. The Review widget lets you practise without opening the app.

A word looks or sounds wrong

Meanings come from Wiktionary, written by volunteers. If a recording sounds poor, tap ••• on the word and choose Report Bad Audio, and Verbo will use a different voice. If a meaning or example is wrong, choose Report a Mistake to tell us. To stop seeing a word, choose Not for Me.

Words aren’t loading

New words need an internet connection. To learn offline, open Settings and tap Download for Offline while you’re connected.

Get in touch

Questions, problems or ideas: we read every message and reply as soon as we can.
