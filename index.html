Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

Verbo works without an account. We don’t run ads or analytics, and we don’t operate a server that stores your learning history.

Information we handle

Your settings, saved words, practice history and streak are kept on your device. Verbo’s widgets read the shared app data needed to show your words and progress.

iCloud Sync is enabled by default. When available, your saved words, review progress and learning statistics sync through your private iCloud account so they can appear on your Apple devices. Apple operates iCloud; Verbo’s developer cannot access your private CloudKit database. Apple’s privacy policy applies to its services.

Dictionary services

To find words, definitions, examples and pronunciations, the app connects directly to:

  • Wiktionary and Wikimedia Commons, operated by the Wikimedia Foundation. The word requested is sent to them.
  • GitHub, to retrieve public frequency lists used to choose words.

These services receive the normal connection information, such as your device’s IP address, and apply their own privacy policies. Verbo does not send them your name, email address, saved-word list or practice history.

Speech and microphone

The microphone is used only when you choose Speak practice and grant microphone access. Apple’s Speech framework checks what you say. Depending on your device, language and settings, Apple may process speech on-device or using its services. Verbo does not record or retain your voice on its own servers. See Apple’s privacy information.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled by iOS on your device. Verbo does not send your reminder schedule to its own server.

Purchases

Apple processes App Store purchases and subscriptions. Verbo uses StoreKit to check whether Premium features are available. We do not receive your payment card details or Apple Account identity. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Retention and control

You can manage saved words and learning history in Verbo. You can turn off iCloud Sync in the app’s Settings or manage Verbo’s iCloud data through Apple’s iCloud settings. Removing the app removes its local app data; iCloud data is managed separately by Apple.

Messages to support

If you choose to email us, your email app opens with a draft. We receive your address and the message only if you send it. Verbo adds its app version and your iOS version to the draft; word reports may also include the word, language and meaning shown so we can investigate. We do not receive drafts you discard.

Children

Verbo does not ask for a name, email address or account. It is not designed to collect personal information from children. Dictionary entries are user-contributed and can vary in subject matter.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the policy shown in the app, along with the revision date.

Support

Questions, feature ideas or a word to report? Email verboapp.team@outlook.com. Verbo opens a message in your email app; you choose whether to send it.

For subscription billing, refunds or cancellation, use Apple’s Report a Problem and subscription settings.

Apple’s Standard EULA

A note on word sources

Dictionary content and recordings are contributed to Wiktionary and Wikimedia Commons under their respective open licences. Attribution and source details are available in Verbo under Settings → Credits and on word entries where available.
